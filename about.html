1. Understanding the House Edge

Every game—whether it is Rummy, Slots, or Fantasy Sports—is designed with a mathematical advantage for the operator. While skill can play a role, the "house" always maintains a margin.

  • The Reality of Variance: Winning streaks can end abruptly, and losing streaks are a normal part of the statistical cycle.
  • Entertainment Only: Never view gaming as a primary source of income or a way to pay off debt.

2. Identifying the Warning Signs

Healthy gaming is controlled and fun. It becomes a problem when it begins to interfere with your daily life. Ask yourself the following:

  • Are you spending money that was intended for essentials like rent or groceries?
  • Do you find yourself "chasing losses" by playing longer to win back what you lost?
  • Does gaming cause you to feel anxious, irritable, or secretive with your family?
  • Have you ever borrowed money or sold assets to fund your gaming account?

If you answered "yes" to any of these, it may be time to take a step back.

3. Practical Tools for Control

  • Deposit Limits: Cap the amount you can transfer daily, weekly, or monthly.
  • Session Timers: Set alerts for play duration.
  • Cool-off Periods: Take breaks when needed.
  • Self-Exclusion: Block access for long-term control.
