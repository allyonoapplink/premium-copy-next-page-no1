Contact Yono Games Site

At Yono Games Site, we prioritize open communication and transparency. Whether you have a technical question about an app we've reviewed, need clarification about our data verification process, or want to report a discrepancy, our team is here to help.

We aim to respond to all legitimate inquiries within 24 to 48 hours.

How Can We Assist You?

  • General Inquiries: Questions about our platform, content, or how we use data.
  • App Verification & Feedback: If you have experienced issues with an app listed on our site (such as withdrawal delays or technical bugs), let us know so our Research Lab can investigate.
  • Partnerships & Business: For verified operators looking to discuss licensing standards or collaborations.
  • Responsible Gaming Support: If you need help finding resources for self-exclusion or gaming control.

Important Notice

Regarding Third-Party Apps: Yono Games Site is an independent research and distribution hub. We do not manage accounts, payments, or internal technical support for the applications listed on our site.

If you are facing a payment or login issue within a specific app, please contact that app's official support team directly.

However, if you believe a platform is acting unfairly, please report it to us so we can update our Trust Index for the community.
